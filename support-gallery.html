TOPIC 01 / 03

Wildlife & nature

Different native animals and ecosystems. Both countries must protect biodiversity.

🇦🇺 AUSTRALIA

Koalas and eucalyptus forests

Australia has famous native animals such as koalas and kangaroos. Koalas depend on suitable habitats and eucalyptus trees.

“If we protect native habitats, wildlife will have a safer future.”

🇨🇴 COLOMBIA

Andean bears and mountain forests

The spectacled bear lives in the Andes, including Colombia. The country's forests and native plants need conservation.

“If we protect forests, native animals will have safer homes.”

TOPIC 02 / 03

Gastronomy

Each country has traditional dishes connected to daily life and cultural identity.

🇦🇺 AUSTRALIA

Meat pies and lamingtons

Meat pies are a well-known savory food in Australia. Lamingtons are a popular sweet dessert.

“You should try an Australian meat pie.”

🇨🇴 COLOMBIA

Arepas and bandeja paisa

Colombian gastronomy is diverse. Arepas, bandeja paisa and coffee are examples of popular regional food and drink.

“You should try a Colombian arepa.”

TOPIC 03 / 03

Tourism & culture

Famous places help us discover the history, architecture and traditions of each country.

🇦🇺 AUSTRALIA

Sydney Opera House

The Sydney Opera House is an important cultural landmark. Australian culture also includes the traditions of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

“If I visited Sydney, I would see the Opera House.”

🇨🇴 COLOMBIA

Cartagena de Indias

Cartagena has a famous historic center, colonial architecture and Caribbean traditions. It is one of Colombia's tourist destinations.

“If I visited Cartagena, I would explore the old city.”
