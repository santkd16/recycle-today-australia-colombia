SIMILAR TOPIC 01

Fauna & flora

Talk about biodiversity, native animals and representative plants.

🇦🇺 Australia

Koalas, kangaroos and golden wattle

Australia is famous for animals such as koalas and kangaroos. The golden wattle is its national floral emblem.

You can say: “Australia has unique wildlife and protected natural habitats.”

🇨🇴 Colombia

Bird diversity and the Cattleya orchid

Colombia is one of the most biodiverse countries in the world. It is known for many bird species and the national flower, the Cattleya trianae orchid.

You can say: “Colombia stands out for its biodiversity and beautiful ecosystems.”

SIMILAR TOPIC 02

Gastronomy

Talk about traditional food and what people usually eat.

🇦🇺 Australia

Meat pies and lamingtons

Australians often enjoy meat pies as a popular savory food, and lamingtons are a classic sweet dessert.

You can say: “You should try a traditional meat pie in Australia.”

🇨🇴 Colombia

Arepas, bandeja paisa and coffee

Colombian food is very diverse. Many people enjoy arepas, bandeja paisa, and Colombian coffee.

You can say: “Colombian gastronomy is varied and very traditional.”

SIMILAR TOPIC 03

Tourist places

Talk about iconic landmarks and destinations.

🇦🇺 Australia

Sydney Opera House and the Great Ocean Road

The Sydney Opera House is one of the most famous places in Australia. Another popular destination is the Great Ocean Road.

You can say: “Australia attracts visitors with its coastal views and famous architecture.”

🇨🇴 Colombia

Cartagena and Monserrate

Cartagena is a historic tourist destination, and Monserrate is one of the best-known places in Bogotá.

You can say: “Colombia offers historic, cultural and mountain destinations.”

SIMILAR TOPIC 04

Culture & traditions

Talk about cultural identity, traditions and celebrations.

🇦🇺 Australia

First Nations traditions

Australia has rich First Nations traditions, languages and cultural expressions that are important to national identity.

You can say: “Australia is multicultural and it values its First Nations cultures.”

🇨🇴 Colombia

Carnival and cultural diversity

Colombia is known for its musical and cultural diversity, including celebrations such as the Barranquilla Carnival.

You can say: “Colombia expresses its cultural diversity through music, festivals and traditions.”
